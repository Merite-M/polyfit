# PolyFit Brand & Design System (Master Constitution)

**Product:** PolyFit  
**Descriptor:** Corporate Wellness Network  
**Positioning:** B2B2C Corporate Wellness Infrastructure connecting employers, employees, and independent wellness providers across East Africa.  
**Core Motto:** *One Benefit. Multiple Providers. Healthier Teams.*

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Essence

PolyFit is **enterprise wellness infrastructure, NOT gym management software or consumer fitness influencer tech**.

PolyFit operates at the intersection of:
1. **Institutional Trust (Stripe-grade precision):** Dependable, tax-compliant (RRA EBM), mathematically verifiable billing and attendance reconciliation.
2. **Kinetic Human Vitality:** Energetic, vibrant, and positive without becoming an aggressive bodybuilding cliché.
3. **Network Connection:** A frictionless mesh routing corporate wellness benefits to diverse independent venues (gyms, pools, yoga studios, wellness clinics).

### Personality Matrix
- **Tone:** Confident · Precise · Human · Kinetic · Architectural · Clear
- **Visual Feel:** Structured, spacious, high-contrast, luminous accents on deep navy surfaces.
- **Audience Alignment:**
  - *HR & Procurement Execs:* Clear ROI, employee retention, consolidated 18% VAT invoicing.
  - *Employees & Beneficiaries:* Freedom of choice, instant QR check-in, motivating health progress.
  - *Wellness Providers:* Guaranteed on-time bank settlement, zero hardware lock-in, incremental foot traffic.

## 2. Brand Identity & Logo Architecture

The PolyFit logo system is engineered around two symbiotic visual assets:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              THE POLYFIT SYSTEM               │
├───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┤
│    PRIMARY CORPORATE  │    SECONDARY DIGITAL  │
│       IDENTITY        │       NETWORK MOTIF   │
│                       │                       │
│     [3D Isometric     │    [Connected Constel-│
│    Hexagonal 'P']     │     lation Nodes 'P'] │
│                       │                       │
│ Corporate Identity,   │ Dynamic Check-in Pass,│
│ Portals, Facades,     │ Interactive Graph Map,│
│ Executive Collateral  │ Telemetry & Background│
└───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 The Primary Mark: 3D Isometric Hexagonal 'P'
- **Concept Equation:** `[Network Hexagon / Multiple Providers]` + `[Letter 'P']` + `[Open Flow Portal]` = **PolyFit Mark**.
- **Geometry & Facets:**
  - 30° true axonometric / isometric projection.
  - **Clean Open Counter:** The center loop of the 'P' features a clean, unencumbered hexagonal negative space (counter) — NO internal multifaceted clutter.
  - **Facet Shading & Lighting:**
    - *Top Horizontal Plane:* Radiant PolyFit Electric Green (`#28D17C`).
    - *Left Vertical Outer Plane:* Dynamic Emerald (`#22C55E` / `#10B981`).
    - *Right Outer Angled Plane:* Deep Emerald Tone (`#00A86B`).
    - *Inner Drop Shadow / Cavity Plane:* PolyFit Midnight Navy (`#0B1F33`) creating tactile spatial depth.
- **Wordmark Typography:**
  - Set in **Inter Bold (700)** with `-0.02em` tracking.
  - Two-tone brand treatment: **Poly** (Midnight Navy `#0B1F33` in light mode, Pure White `#FFFFFF` in dark mode) + **Fit** (PolyFit Electric Green `#28D17C`).
- **Descriptor Line:**
  - `CORPORATE WELLNESS NETWORK` in Inter SemiBold (600), uppercase, tracked at `+0.12em`, accompanied by subtle horizontal flanking hairline rules.

### 2.2 The Secondary Digital Motif: Constellation Connected Nodes
- A constellation graph forming the letter 'P' with 14 luminous circular vertices connected by 1.75px vector lines.
- **Role:** Interactive QR check-in pass, mobile app pulse animation, graph telemetry visualization in HR dashboards, and subtle grid backgrounds.

### 3.2 Gradient Ramps (Network Momentum)
1. **Kinetic Network Flow:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #28D17C 0%, #00D2B4 100%)`
   - Used for interactive pass glow, graph trends, and hero highlights.
2. **Vitality Boost:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #28D17C 0%, #B8F36B 100%)`
   - Used for high-engagement metrics and milestone achievements.
3. **Deep Executive Surface:** `linear-gradient(180deg, #0D2235 0%, #071521 100%)`
   - Used for high-contrast dark dashboard cards and kiosk monitors.

### 3.3 Strict Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA & AAA)
- `#28D17C` on `#0B1F33` provides a **9.8:1 contrast ratio** (surpasses WCAG AAA).
- `#0B1F33` on `#FFFFFF` provides a **16.1:1 contrast ratio** (surpasses WCAG AAA).
- `#FFFFFF` on `#28D17C` has a **2.2:1 contrast ratio** (FAIL for body copy). **RULE:** Never place white text on `#28D17C`. Always use Midnight Navy `#0B1F33` text on PolyFit Green buttons!

## 5. Shape, Spacing & Elevation Architecture

### 5.1 Border Radius Constants
- **Content Cards & Panels:** `14px` (`rounded-xl` in standard Tailwind, `var(--pf-radius-card)`)
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** `10px` (`var(--pf-radius-btn)`)
- **Nested Inner Items:** `8px` (`var(--pf-radius-inner)`)
- **Chips, Badges & Avatars:** `9999px` (`rounded-full`)

### 5.2 Elevation & Shadows
PolyFit avoids heavy fuzzy drop shadows. Surfaces communicate hierarchy through **tonal borders and ambient diffusions**:
- **Hairline Border (Default):** `1px solid #E2E8F0` on light canvas; `1px solid #21405A` on dark canvas.
- **Card Ambient Shadow:** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.03)`
- **Hover Elevation:** `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.04)`
- **Modal / Floating Dropdown:** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.15), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 31, 51, 0.1)`

## 7. Actor-Specific Visual Dialects

PolyFit serves distinct actors; each interface inherits the core tokens with purposeful hierarchy:

### 1. Corporate Employer (B2B HR Portal)
- **Visual Weight:** Executive, analytical, institutional.
- **Dominant Palette:** Crisp White surfaces, Deep Navy typography, Kinetic Teal and PolyFit Green for utilization graphs.
- **Key Modules:** Active employee rosters, RRA-compliant 18% VAT invoices, monthly benefit utilization charts, budget forecasting.

### 2. Beneficiary / Employee (Mobile & Web Pass)
- **Visual Weight:** Kinetic, personal, rewarding, frictionless.
- **Dominant Palette:** Deep Navy header with radiant Green dynamic TOTP QR code, venue discovery cards with real imagery, category filter chips.
- **Key Modules:** Dynamic QR pass, near me facility map, monthly visit counter, co-pay wallet balance.

### 3. Wellness Provider (Partner Console)
- **Visual Weight:** Operational, rapid, high legibility.
- **Dominant Palette:** Clean high-density tables, instant green check-in validation feedback, transparent settlement calculations.
- **Key Modules:** Live visit feed, manual PIN entry, dispute queue, monthly settlement payout reports.

### 4. PolyFit Operations (Super Admin)
- **Visual Weight:** Infrastructure console, network health telemetry.
- **Dominant Palette:** System metrics, node connectivity graphs, organization and provider lifecycle onboarding wizards.

## 8. Physical Collateral & Real-World Guidelines

As established in Brand Board 1 & 2:
1. **Architectural Signage:** Brushed titanium/aluminum or matte black acrylic letters with face-illuminated PolyFit Electric Green (`#28D17C`) letters and 3D isometric P on dark glass facades.
2. **Business Stationery:** 400gsm soft-touch cotton cardstock in PolyFit Midnight Navy (`#0B1F33`) with spot-UV gloss emerald logo on the front, and crisp pure white on the reverse.
3. **Hardware / Merch:** Matte black thermal water bottles, branded NFC reception tap-tags, modern staff lanyards with the 3D isometric P symbol.
4. **Photography:** Real African corporate teams, diverse professionals, genuine athletic venues in Kigali and East Africa (running, swimming, weight training, group yoga). Zero artificial studio handshakes or bodybuilding extremes.

## 9. Non-Negotiable Anti-Patterns (The 18 Directives)

1. ❌ **NEVER** use pure black (`#000000`). Always use PolyFit Midnight Navy (`#0B1F33`).
2. ❌ **NEVER** place white text on PolyFit Green (`#28D17C`). Contrast is insufficient. Always use Midnight Navy (`#0B1F33`) text.
3. ❌ **NEVER** introduce purple, hot pink, orange, or AI-style neon gradients.
4. ❌ **NEVER** use the legacy gym software terms: "Gym Member", "Gym Membership", "Gym POS", "Attendance". Always use "Beneficiary", "Benefit", "Verified Visit", "Provider".
5. ❌ **NEVER** leave a multifaceted solid block inside the loop counter of the letter 'P'. The center counter of the 'P' is clean hexagonal negative space.
6. ❌ **NEVER** use arbitrary border radii. Cards are strictly `14px`, buttons/inputs are strictly `10px`, badges are `9999px`.
7. ❌ **NEVER** use generic 3-equal-card SaaS feature rows. Prefer asymmetric split sections and data-rich tables.
8. ❌ **NEVER** use emojis in the professional UI. Use Lucide icons exclusively with `1.75px` stroke.
9. ❌ **NEVER** invent fake review testimonials or fabricated metrics (e.g. "1,000,000 checkins"). Use authentic metrics.
10. ❌ **NEVER** use glassmorphism with heavy blur as default container styling.
11. ❌ **NEVER** use fonts other than **Inter** (UI) and **JetBrains Mono** (Code/Numbers).
12. ❌ **NEVER** distort, stretch, or rotate the 3D isometric hexagonal 'P'.
13. ❌ **NEVER** make a gym the center of the architecture. PolyFit is an aggregator across gyms, pools, yoga studios, and clinics.
14. ❌ **NEVER** use buzzwords like "Revolutionize", "Unleash", "Next-Gen AI" in UI copy.
15. ❌ **NEVER** apply drop shadows to the logo mark.
16. ❌ **NEVER** create competing primary buttons on the same view.
17. ❌ **NEVER** omit 18% VAT or RRA compliance from corporate invoice specifications.
18. ❌ **NEVER** use low-contrast muted gray for active interactive states.
