# PolyFit Business Model

## Executive Summary

PolyFit operates as a B2B2C corporate wellness network, connecting employers with a network of independent wellness providers. The platform manages the entire lifecycle from employer contracts to provider settlements, creating value through aggregation, verification, and efficient administration.

---

## Value Proposition

### For Employers

**Problem Solved:**
- Fragmented wellness provider relationships
- Inconsistent benefit administration
- Lack of utilization visibility
- Complex billing across multiple providers
- Difficulty measuring wellness ROI

**Value Delivered:**
- Single contract for multiple providers
- Centralized benefit administration
- Real-time utilization tracking
- Consolidated billing
- Comprehensive reporting and analytics
- Measurable wellness ROI

### For Employees

**Problem Solved:**
- Limited wellness options (single gym)
- Inconvenient provider locations
- Complex access procedures
- Lack of benefit visibility
- Inconsistent experience across providers

**Value Delivered:**
- Network of provider options
- Convenient location choices
- Simple access verification
- Clear benefit information
- Consistent experience

### For Providers

**Problem Solved:**
- Inconsistent corporate client acquisition
- Complex billing and reconciliation

- Revenue unpredictability

**Value Delivered:**

## Revenue Model

### Primary Revenue Streams

#### 1. Platform Subscription Fees

**Model:** Employers pay monthly/annual platform subscription

**Pricing Structure:**
- Small (50-200 employees): $X/month
- Medium (200-500 employees): $Y/month
- Large (500+ employees): $Z/month

**Value-Based Pricing:**
- Platform access
- Benefit administration
- Utilization reporting
- Support services

#### 2. Transaction/Usage Fees

**Model:** Per-transaction margin on verified visits

**Pricing Structure:**
- Per verified visit: $A per visit
- Percentage of provider rate: B% margin
- Tiered volume discounts

**Value Proposition:**
- Pay for actual usage
- Scalable with growth
- Aligns incentives

#### 3. Provider Settlement Fees

**Model:** Processing fees on provider settlements

**Pricing Structure:**
- Per settlement: $C per settlement
- Percentage of settlement: D% processing fee
- Volume-based discounts

**Value Proposition:**
- Automated settlement processing
- Reduced provider admin burden
- Faster payment cycles

#### 4. Premium Features (Future)

**Model:** Additional fees for advanced features

**Potential Offerings:**
- Advanced analytics dashboards
- Wellness challenges and programs
- Integration with HR systems
- Custom reporting
- API access for enterprise clients

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Enterprise:** $X (direct sales)
**Mid-Market:** $Y (digital marketing)
**SME:** $Z (channel partners)

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Enterprise:** $A (3-year contract)
**Mid-Market:** $B (2-year contract)
**SME:** $C (1-year contract)

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**Target:** 3:1 or higher
**Enterprise:** 4:1 (high retention)
**Mid-Market:** 3:1 (moderate retention)
**SME:** 2:1 (lower retention)

### Gross Margin

**Platform Fees:** 80-90%
**Transaction Fees:** 60-70%
**Settlement Fees:** 40-50%

### Net Margin

**Target:** 20-30% at scale
**Early Stage:** Negative (investment phase)
**Break-even:** 12-18 months post-launch

## Risk Factors

### Market Risks

**Risk:** Low employer demand for corporate wellness
**Mitigation:** Validate demand before investment, start with pilot

**Risk:** Provider resistance to network model
**Mitigation:** Flexible terms, guaranteed minimums, clear value

**Risk:** Competitive pressure from global players
**Mitigation:** Regional focus, local relationships, specialized features

### Operational Risks

**Risk:** Visit verification challenges
**Mitigation:** Multiple verification methods, provider confirmation

**Risk:** Settlement disputes and fraud
**Mitigation:** Clear contracts, audit trails, dispute resolution process

**Risk:** Technology scalability issues
**Mitigation:** Cloud infrastructure, load testing, monitoring

### Financial Risks

**Risk:** Longer sales cycles than expected
**Mitigation:** Diversified acquisition channels, pipeline management

**Risk:** Higher customer acquisition costs
**Mitigation:** Improve marketing efficiency, referral programs

**Risk:** Cash flow constraints
**Mitigation:** Recurring revenue model, efficient operations
